# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML, CSS and vanilla JS (index.html, style.css, script.js). No build step. Hosted from the GitHub repository Mtreck/Site-Antonio-Diniz.

## Users

- Families and the local community who want to see how the school fair went.
- The school's outward communication: the page is shared on social media and shown to people outside the school to present the college's work.
- Students contribute texts to the site through pull requests; their contributions must be kept and credited in the git history.

## Product Purpose

A retrospective landing page for the 2025 edition of the Feira "Ser, Saber e Empreender" at the Colégio Estadual Cívico-Militar Antonio Diniz Pereira (Paraná state network). It records that the fair happened, explains why it matters to the students, shows the photos, and invites visitors to follow the school on Instagram. Success: a first-time visitor understands within seconds what fair this is, which school, when, and can browse the photos.

## Positioning

It is the school's own record of its own fair, partly written by its students. Only facts the school confirmed are shown.

## Operating Context

- Fair dates: 27 and 28 November 2025 (abertura and visitação). Confirmed by the user after a student correction.
- Opened mostly on phones from Instagram and WhatsApp links.
- Original photos and videos of the fair live in fotos/2025/ (not committed). Only selected items are copied to fotos/ with readable names and published.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Two-day structure: day 1 Abertura, day 2 Visitação.
- A single gallery of photos and short muted videos (no split by day: the files do not say which day they were taken), with lightbox.
- Only photos and videos without identifiable students may be published (user decision, 2026-11-03).
- Instagram block in the middle of the page invites visitors to post photos and videos of the fair and tag @ccmantoniodiniz, so the tagged posts can build the next edition's page; the link also sits in the footer: https://www.instagram.com/ccmantoniodiniz/
- Open decisions: no confirmed schedule, project list, participant counts, or testimonials.

## Brand Commitments

- Official name: Colégio Estadual Cívico-Militar Antonio Diniz Pereira (written "Antonio" without accent, as given by the user).
- Fair name: Feira "Ser, Saber e Empreender", Edição 2025.
- Official crest of the Colégios Cívico-Militares do Paraná at img/brasao.webp must appear.
- Palette must follow the civic-military uniform tones: navy blue, light blue shirt, green stripe (also the Paraná flag green).
- Language: Brazilian Portuguese.

## Evidence on Hand

- img/brasao.webp: official CCM-PR crest.
- Student-written texts merged from PRs #1, #2, #3, #5, #6, #7 (lightly edited for grammar, meaning kept).
- Published media in fotos/: 6 photos of projects and 3 short videos, chosen because no student face is visible.
- Absent and never to be fabricated: number of projects, project names, visitor counts, schedule times, quotes from staff or students.

## Product Principles

1. Say only what the school confirmed; when information is missing, leave it out instead of inventing.
2. The students' voice stays in the page; edits polish their words without replacing them.
3. The page must be understood at first glance on a phone.
4. The school identity (crest and uniform colors) is always visible.
