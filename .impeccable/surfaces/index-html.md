# Surface brief: index.html (landing page da feira)

Scope: the single landing page. Visitor mode: Persuade. Audience: families, local community, and outward school communication, mostly on phones from Instagram and WhatsApp. Job: understand which fair, which school, when, and browse the photos; secondary action: follow @ccmantoniodiniz. Proof: the crest, the confirmed dates (27 and 28 Nov 2025), real photos (pending), student-written texts. Constraints: no invented numbers, schedules, quotes or project names.

Memorable moment: the white diagonal band of the Paraná flag sweeps across the green field carrying the fair's name, and the blue sphere with the Cruzeiro do Sul settles on it holding the crest.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is built on the Paraná state flag's geometry, the civic emblem every family in the state knows, instead of a landing-page template. It refuses the split hero with copy beside an image and the row of equal cards.

OWN-WORLD: Flag green field (deep #006B38 for text grounds, flag green #00A651 as signal), white band, sphere blue #0B4EA2 with white five-point stars in Cruzeiro do Sul arrangement, yellow #F5C400 rationed to actions only. Flat colour, no gradients, no glass. One diagonal angle (the band's rise, lower-left to upper-right) governs band, section cuts and rules. Wide heavy civic sans for display, a hyperlegible sans for text.

STORY: The visitor sees the fair's name on the flag band and knows it is a Paraná state school; reads that it happened on 27 and 28 November 2025; understands why it matters through the students' own texts; browses the photos by day; follows the school on Instagram.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed green field. Header on green: crest and school name left, links right. A white band crosses the whole viewport rising to the right, carrying "Feira" and "Ser, Saber e Empreender" in navy at display scale. The blue sphere sits on the band at the right holding the crest, five stars around it. Below the band on green: the lead sentence with dates and the yellow "Ver as fotos" button.

FORM: Bandeira do Paraná, position 3 of 7 on the grounded list; seed:a547fc64fbdc8106. Signature interaction: on load the band sweeps in along its diagonal, the sphere settles, the five stars light one by one; in the gallery, photos develop in once as the section enters.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
